Privacy Policy for Clipboarder

Last updated: October 2026

Clipboarder is a Chrome extension that lets you collect multiple text selections into a local list and paste them back using two keyboard shortcuts you configure yourself. This page explains exactly what the extension does and does not do with your data.

Short version

Clipboarder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data, ever. It has no servers, makes no network requests, and has no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What data the extension touches

  • Text you select on a page, but only while you have explicitly turned on "copy mode" with your own keyboard shortcut. Outside of that mode, the extension does not read page content.
  • Settings you choose (hotkey behavior, item/character limits, formatting preferences).

Where that data goes

Everything is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), on your own device. It is never sent to any external server, because the extension doesn't have one. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing its data from chrome://extensions, permanently deletes everything it has stored.

Why the extension requests broad page access

Clipboarder requests access to all websites (<all_urls>) solely because its copy/paste shortcuts need to work on whichever page you're currently using - there's no way to predict in advance which site that will be. The extension only acts on a page's content when you explicitly trigger it via your configured shortcuts or by clicking an item in its popup.

Third parties

Clipboarder does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party service, advertiser, or analytics provider.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
