Clipboarder

Press the copy hotkey to turn on copy mode (a small arrow badge appears on the page), then select text normally to add it to this list. Items stay here after pasting so you can reuse them. Click an item to select it and paste it where your cursor is; the item marked ➤ is the selected one. The paste hotkey always repeats that same item - click a different item to change what it pastes. Long selections get trimmed and marked cut; formatted copies show rich. Use ⚙ Settings to change hotkeys, limits and formatting options.
